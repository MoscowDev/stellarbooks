# Open Questions

Unresolved questions, ambiguities in upstream documentation, and anything deliberately deferred or
stubbed.

**Status values:**

- `OPEN`: needs an answer.
- `ANSWERED`: an answer is recorded, but it may still need work.
- `DEFERRED`: intentionally postponed, with a reason.

**Where questions live:** this file holds the project-level and decision questions. The detailed
documentation gaps found during research stay in the "Open questions" section of each research note,
under their original IDs, and are indexed at the bottom. A research question moves here when it blocks
a milestone.

| Q-040 | CSV formula-injection handling. `toCsv` now prefixes formula-like text cells with `'` by default and exempts plain decimal numbers such as `-100`. OWASP does not address numeric cells, and its single-quote mitigation is not reliable in Excel after save and reopen. Should the default be different (for example a tab prefix, or opt-in escaping)? | OPEN (non-blocking; current default documented in research/report-exports.md) |
| Q-041 | Text memos are bytes and may not be valid UTF-8. The decoder keeps invalid text in the reversible SEP-51 escape form (`XdrString.toJson`) as a `text` memo. Should `core` carry a separate byte form instead?                                                                                                                                            | OPEN (non-blocking)                                                           |
| Q-042 | A `ClaimableBalanceEntry` does not record its creator, so a stateless classifier cannot tell a creator reclaiming from a release to another claimant. The earlier classifier booked `release` for every other tracked wallet, which was wrong. Claims now go to suspense until they are resolved against the book's own `lock` by balance ID (issue 12). | OPEN                                                                          |
| Q-043 | ADR-0008 asks for the Soroban fee charge and refund as separate events. The classifier books one net fee entry for `feeCharged`. The decoder verifies the net, so totals are exact, but the gross charge and refund are not shown (issue 05).                                                                                                            | OPEN (documented deviation)                                                   |
