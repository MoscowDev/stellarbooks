# Open Questions

Unresolved questions, ambiguities in upstream documentation, and anything deliberately deferred or
stubbed.

**Status values:**

- `OPEN`: needs an answer.
- `ANSWERED`: an answer is recorded, but it may still need work.
- `DEFERRED`: intentionally postponed, with a reason.

**Where questions live:** this file holds the project-level and decision questions. The detailed
documentation gaps found during research stay in the "Open questions" section of each research note,
under their original IDs, and are indexed at the bottom. A research question moves here when it blocks
a milestone.
